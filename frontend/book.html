Check any entry yourself

Nothing here needs to be taken on trust. Every row links to its transactions on a public explorer; the book account is ….

  1. Fetch an entry: curl -s https://usetouchmark.app/api/v1/assay/entry/<entry>
  2. Hash its entry object as canonical JSON (sorted keys, no spaces):
python3 -c "import sys,json,hashlib;e=json.load(sys.stdin)['entry'];print(hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(e,sort_keys=True,separators=(',',':'),ensure_ascii=False).encode()).hexdigest())"
  1. Open the entry's stamp transaction: its memo is that hash, sent from the book account at the time of the audit.
  2. For a paid report, the report JSON hashes to the entry's report_sha256, the payer's payment carries the memo tm: + the first 20 hex of the entry, and the receipt stamp sent back to the payer carries the entry hash.
