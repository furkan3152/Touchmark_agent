      Can I add any Stellar agent?

      You can submit a public G-account or C-contract address on Testnet or Mainnet, or provide source without a wallet. The address check is read-only. A private agent needs its owner's source or a later authorized runtime test; no service can fully audit hidden behavior from an address alone.

      Does Stellar have an agent key?

      There is no universal agent ID required by this product. Agents may use ordinary Stellar accounts or smart accounts with delegated permissions. We ask for public identifiers only. A private key is authority to act, not evidence of trustworthy behavior.

      What does the current audit prove?

      Seven rule-based dimensions inspect the supplied evidence. They do not execute your agent. The report distinguishes source findings from missing evidence. Owner signatures and successful Soroban state readback are required for a published audit record; this screen does not claim that step is complete.
