AI hypothesis · main.py:25
Lack of transaction atomicity or external payment confirmation leads to double payment risk on restart
The payment is submitted by calling self.chain.pay without explicit persistent state updates before or after payment, relying solely on in-memory self.completed set. This means if process restarts happen after payment submission but before persisting the invoice as completed, duplicate payments may occur.
Persist the payment completion status of each invoice in durable storage outside process memory before or immediately after calling pay, to enable idempotent behavior across restarts.
AI hypothesis · service.py:24
KeyError risk when supplier not found in approved suppliers dictionary
Direct dictionary access suppliers[item['supplier']] can raise KeyError if the batch invoice contains unapproved supplier keys, causing unhandled exceptions and potential service disruption.
Add explicit existence checks for supplier keys before access and handle missing supplier cases gracefully with error responses.
